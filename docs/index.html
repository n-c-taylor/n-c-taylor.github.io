I am a fourth-year PhD candidate in the Department of Statistical Science at Baylor University, advised by Dr. Amanda S. Hering. I am interested in spatial and spatiotemporal statistical modeling, predictive model validation, satellite remote sensing, and functional data analysis, with applications to water resources and the environmental and biological sciences. My work is motivated by applied problems, and I enjoy collaborative research that brings statistical methods to bear on real-world questions.

I am a Research Assistant with the Baylor Water Security Collaborative, where I am working to characterize public water data resources across Texas.

Research interests

  • Spatial and spatiotemporal statistics
  • Predictive model validation
  • Satellite remote sensing
  • Functional data analysis
  • Applications in water resources and the environmental sciences
